## What OSForensics Is

OSForensics is a case workspace for investigators who are authorized to review a computer and need files, artifacts, and notes in one place. OSForensics file artifact review gathers items for the case, and OSForensics drive investigation ties those items to the drive that was examined. You open OSForensics for a system you have permission to study, then keep a record of what you looked at.

OSForensics evidence notes sit beside the files they describe, so a later reader can see why an item was saved. The OSForensics case workspace holds the scope of the review: which drive, which folders, and which notes belong to that matter. The work is an examination you were asked to perform, with a clear trail of what the authorized review covered.

## Practical Notes for OSForensics

Limit the OSForensics case to the computer and the folders named in your authorization. Write evidence notes in plain language so another reviewer can see what was examined and why it was saved. If a path is outside that scope, leave it out of the drive investigation.

OSForensics is for a review you are allowed to perform. Keep the case workspace, the file artifact review, and the evidence notes aligned with that permission, and close the case when the examination record is complete.
